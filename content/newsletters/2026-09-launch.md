# We're live

Amplify 850 officially launched this fall, aligning Florida State University and Leon
County Schools around one shared cause: fine arts education.

## What's on the calendar

- **October 2** — Dance Leadership Seminar, FSU Moore Auditorium
- **November 7** — Choral Leadership Seminar, held by the American Choral Directors Association
- **December 5** — Acting Leadership Seminar
- **February 2027** — the Fine Arts & Music Conference, our grand finale

All three fall seminars are free. Details and RSVPs are on the [Events page](https://www.amplify850.org/events).

## Get involved

Whether you're an FSU student, a Leon County parent or educator, or just someone who
wants to see Tallahassee's young artists succeed — there's a seat for you. [Sign up
here](https://www.amplify850.org/join).
